A portable data contract for Aspen results

Version 1 · steady-state rates · normalization to 1 kg declared target product. No HTL kinetics, operating range or background factor is imposed on imported projects.

Start in three steps

  1. Export numerical results from Aspen as values-only Excel/CSV, or import a version-1 JSON project. Use the blank workbook for the required field names. Native .bkp/.apw files cannot be simulated in the browser.
  2. Review the suggested column matches. Select the worksheet and target table, choose a header row, and transpose property rows if necessary. Confirm units, topology, Q/W signs, product basis and factor sources.
  3. Calculate. Inspect missing-data messages and balance residuals before using impact results or ranking. Save the project and result JSON; local uploads disappear on reload.

Five tables

  • Cases: id, product_rate, product_low/product_high (optional triangular support), feed_rate, mass_unit, allocation, source. Product and feed rates share the declared mass unit. Allocation is an explicit 0–1 burden share; use 1 when no co-products are allocated. Yield = target product rate / declared feed rate.
  • Streams: case_id, id, from, to, mass_flow, mass_unit, enthalpy_flow, energy_unit, source. Use BOUNDARY for external connections. Alternatively supply specific_enthalpy and enthalpy_unit; all material substreams must be included exactly once. Do not sum TOTAL and its constituent substreams. If both enthalpy forms are supplied, they must agree.
  • Blocks: case_id, id, heat, power, energy_unit, source. Optional heat_unit and power_unit override the shared unit. Heat and shaft work are signed positive INTO each block. Enter a known zero explicitly; blank is unknown. A heat exchanger's internal transfer is not another external duty.
  • Inventory: case_id, id, name, kind, amount, amount_low/amount_high, unit, factor_id, source. Every quantity is per hour. kind is energy, material, emission or waste. Only declared external burdens belong here; recycle streams do not. Convert useful heat to purchased electricity/fuel with documented efficiency and heating value outside this version, then import the actual purchased quantity.
  • Factors: id, category, value, low/high, denominator_unit, impact_unit, method, source. A factor ID can have several categories, one entry per category. For example 0.5 kg CO2-eq/kWh: value=0.5, denominator_unit=kWh, impact_unit=kg CO2-eq. Each category must use one consistent method and impact unit. A missing category factor is never silently zero.

Supported quantities

Mass rates: kg/h, kg/s, kg/min, g/s, g/h, t/h, lb/h, lb/s. Power/enthalpy rates: W, kW, MW, mW, J/s, kJ/s, kJ/h, MJ/h, GJ/h, kWh/h, Btu/h. Specific enthalpy: J/kg, kJ/kg, MJ/kg, Btu/lb. Volume: m³/h, m³/s, L/h. Molar: kmol/h, mol/h, mol/s. hr/sec spellings are recognized. Factor denominators: kg, kWh, MJ, GJ, m³, kmol. Unlisted or dimensionally incompatible units are rejected.

For cal/s, kcal/h, kcal/s, cal/g or kcal/kg, select the convention explicitly: thermochemical 4.184 or International Table 4.1868 J/cal. These differ; they must not be mixed silently. NIST conversion factors.

Balance equations

mass residual = Σ m_out − Σ m_in
energy residual = Σ H_out − Σ H_in − Q_in − W_in
H_rate [kW] = m [kg/h] × h [kJ/kg] / 3600

Assumptions: steady state, common enthalpy reference, complete material substreams, negligible kinetic/potential energy. Reactions are represented in total stream enthalpy; do not add reaction heat again. Every internal stream cancels when taking the system boundary. Signed enthalpy may be negative because its zero is a reference, not an absolute energy content.

Numerical consistency limits are max(0.01 kg/h, 0.1% mass throughput) and max(0.01 kW, 0.1% absolute enthalpy/duty scale), applied per block. Computed residuals remain visible even when these checks fail. They are implementation screening limits, not physical validation or operating-performance thresholds.

Inventory-based impact calculation

impact per kg product = allocation share × Σ(quantity per hour × factor) / product kg per hour

Import upstream factors for feed/utilities and characterization factors for direct emissions consistently with the declared system boundary. Aspen alone does not supply a complete background life-cycle inventory. The calculation can handle multiple supplied categories, but does not connect an LCA database, perform database expansion or certify an ISO-compliant study. Missing factors produce a labelled partial subtotal and block that category's ranking. Functional-unit and inventory documentation follow the separation of inventory and impact assessment in the JRC ILCD guidance.

Two different meanings of fuzzy

Field recognition: aliases plus normalized edit-distance similarity suggest English/Chinese column matches. The score is lexical similarity, not semantic confidence. A human reviews column roles and units before applying them; no values are inferred from a weak match.

Parameter propagation: nonnegative triangular fuzzy inputs (lower, mode, upper) have α-cut [lower + α(mode−lower), upper − α(upper−mode)]. Interval multiplication, addition and division produce bounds at α = 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1. A positive reference-product lower bound is required. These are Cartesian possibility enclosures. They do not encode correlations or measured probability distributions; repeated/shared dependencies can make bounds conservative. They are not confidence intervals. See scikit-fuzzy's triangular membership and alpha-cut definitions.

Multi-criteria evaluation: normalize nominal energy, impact and yield using the current comparison set's nominal min/max; freeze those anchors for support bounds. Lower energy/impact and higher yield define desirability x ∈ [0,1]. Memberships are low=max(0,1−2x), medium=1−|2x−1|, high=max(0,2x−1). Combine them with normalized user weights; score=0.5×medium+high. Scores are relative preferences. Overlapping score supports yield conservative possible rank ranges, not ranking probabilities. Cases need complete numerical balances and complete selected-category inventory; comparability is explicitly confirmed by the user.

Limits to portability

This version does not parse every arbitrary Aspen report layout automatically. The mapping editor is the adapter. The existing repository JSON exporter can be staged, but missing topology or total enthalpy requires an enriched export. Batch/dynamic accumulation, kinetic/potential energy, molar-specific enthalpy without molecular-weight data, negative credits/system expansion, and non-mass functional units are not supported. Internal heat recovery, electricity/steam efficiency, fuel heating values and co-product allocation must be supplied or preprocessed explicitly. A successful import is not model validation.

Software and methodology

Excel support: locally vendored SheetJS CE 0.20.3, Apache 2.0 license supplied. No spreadsheet formulas or macros are executed; formula workbooks must be exported as values-only. Maximum file size 20 MB; 10,000 data rows per worksheet.
