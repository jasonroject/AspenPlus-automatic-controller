Assumptions and interpretation

Independent uniform scenario assumptions, not measured uncertainty distributions. Equal weights favor higher monomer output, lower energy and lower carbon footprint. Ranking scales remain fixed at nominal values. GP prediction error, kinetics and property uncertainty are not propagated.
