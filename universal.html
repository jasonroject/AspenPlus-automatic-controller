Calculation basis

Reference product rates and allocation shares are in the Cases table. Results are per 1 kg target product. Heat and shaft-work signs must be normalized before import; internal heat exchange is not an extra purchased utility.

Inventory and impact factor coverage

Factors need a category, method, unit and source. Every inventory row needs an explicit factor for each evaluated category, including zero where justified.

Aspen duties do not identify a regional electricity mix, steam supplier, fuel supply chain or LCIA method. Import those factors explicitly. Energy inventory uses kind = energy; quantities are per hour. No background database is bundled.

Impact accounting & fuzzy intervals

Triangular inputs → α-cut interval arithmetic. These are possibility bounds, not confidence intervals.

Fuzzy multi-criteria evaluation

Relative low / medium / high desirability memberships use fixed nominal min–max anchors. Energy is purchased-energy inventory per kg product; yield is target/feed mass. No performance threshold. Support-based rank ranges are conservative possibilities, not top-rank probabilities.
